What it does

The capabilitiesthat matter.

F/01

Connector inventory

Every integration that can read your data, listed with its real scope — including the ones somebody added and forgot.

F/02

Personal data in transit

Detects names, emails, numbers and records being forwarded to third-party models without redaction — the leak nobody logs.

F/03

Secrets in the open

API keys in environment variables, notebooks, automation steps and prompt templates, flagged with where to move them.

F/04

Retrieval over-reach

RAG pipelines that return whole documents when a field would do — the quiet cause of most accidental disclosure.

F/05

A diagnostic you can act on

Findings ranked by severity, each with the component at fault and a suggested fix. No 200-page report nobody reads.

F/06

Runs on your side

Sentinel inspects your stack without becoming another place your data lives.
